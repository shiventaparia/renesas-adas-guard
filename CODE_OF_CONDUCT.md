# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community;
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report concerns privately using the reporting path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will not retaliate against anyone who makes a good-faith report.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
